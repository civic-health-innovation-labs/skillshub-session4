The NORTHSTAR data workflow

NORTHSTAR is a fictional study of 99 adults admitted to three hospitals with a new respiratory virus. A trusted third party links it to national mortality data. Inside the TRE the data passes through three layers: bronze (as received, de-identified, never edited), silver (harmonised, every change logged) and gold (linked, versioned as northstar_v12, documented). Projects apply for a subset of gold. All names and data are invented.
